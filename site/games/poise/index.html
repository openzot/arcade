Poise

The sculptor has gone to bed. Five unfinished mobiles sway on the rack, their carvings spilled into a tray — and the gallery doors open at first light.

  • Hang every carving. Drag one from the tray onto any arm; slide it along the arm to choose its reach.
  • Watch the tips. A ghost arrow shows which way an arm would dip before you let go.
  • Level arms lock gold. When every arm of a piece sits true at once, it is finished.
  • Beat the clock. Each piece must hang complete before the opening bell.
