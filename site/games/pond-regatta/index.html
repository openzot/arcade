Pond Regatta

Three laps of the boating lake against Old Bramble, the park’s champion since before you were born.

  • Steer with ←/→, A/D, or hold the arrow pads / drag on the water.
  • Trim the sheet with ↑/↓, W/S, or the TRIM/EASE pads — match the sail to the wind’s eye.
  • No-go zone: point too close to the wind and your sail flaps — bear away a little.
  • Gusts: darker water breathes harder. Cross it and you leap.
  • Round red can → yellow can → green can, cross the line, three times round wins.
