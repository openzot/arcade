Midnight Broth

Ten hungry regulars. One pot of courage.

  • Drop the noodles — Space or the pot button starts the simmer.
  • Fill the ticket — tap the toppings it shows (1–5). Gold tickets want them in order.
  • Strain in the green — hit Space again while the ring is green. Too soon is crunchy, too long burns the pot.
  • Serve with Enter before patience runs out. Three walk-outs and the shutters fall.
