      Cheat!

      Rain on the glass, tea going cold, and three regulars who never forget a lie.

      • The table owes one rank at a time, climbing Ace to King and round again.
      • On your go, lay 1–4 cards face down as that rank — honest or not.
      • Doubt a rival with Call it! before the timer drains. Right call: they take the whole pile and owe you 2d. Wrong call: you take it.
      • Watch their faces — sometimes. A tell is a rumour, not gospel.
      • First hand empty sweeps the pot. Everyone else buys the tea.

      Paused

      The kettle’s on. Nobody peeks at anybody’s cards.

      House rules

      • Lay 1–4 cards face down claiming the rank the table owes. Lying is legal; getting caught is not free.
      • A caught lie costs you the pile plus 2d to the caller. A false call costs the caller the same.
      • The owed rank climbs one each turn and wraps King back to Ace.
      • Tells flicker now and then. Trust them like weather lore.
      • First out takes the pot of 12d.
