Chartwright

The ship’s only chart slipped overboard in the last squall. Six islands stand between you and landfall — and each shows itself through the fog just once.

  1. Glimpse. An island surfaces from the fog for a few heartbeats. Memorise its shape against the compass rose.
  2. Redraw. The fog takes it back. Drag across the vellum to lay down the coastline as you remember it. Undo or clear and try again until the inkwell runs dry.
  3. Commit. Your ink is laid over the true coast and scored for fidelity. Miss the quota and the voyage loses a quill — lose all three and the fleet is lost with it.
