Foldsong

Every crease decides what shows.

  • Fold the sheet in half, edge over edge, until it is one small sealed packet.
  • The commission above the desk tells you which printed face must finish on top - and which must hide just beneath.
  • Every fold flips its half over, so patterns turn face-down and plain kraft turns face-up. Plan the order of your creases.
  • Fewest folds and one clean seal earn three stars. Undo is free; the paper remembers how much you handled it.
