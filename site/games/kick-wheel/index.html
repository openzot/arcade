Kick Wheel

Five commissions stand between dusk and the sealed kiln. Raise the walls true or wear the clay for it.

  • Shape — move the pointer (or ↑/↓) to set the tool's height, then hold the mouse, finger, or SPACE to press the spinning clay toward the dashed blueprint.
  • Walls — pressing thins them. Thinner than the guide and the wall tears; thicker than the guide and the vessel throws heavy, capping its price.
  • Wobble — pressing while the tool races up and down wobbles the piece. Hold C or the Centre button to steady it — but the clock never stops.
  • The shift — fire each commission before the kiln seals. Three torn lumps and the wheel goes cold.
