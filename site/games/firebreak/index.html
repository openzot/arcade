Firebreak

A dry electric storm has dropped embers on the far ridge. The wind is picking up. You have a crew, two tools and until the fire arrives to decide where the valley ends.

  • Cut breaks. Drag across scrub to strip it to bare dirt. Fire cannot cross bare dirt.
  • Burn ahead of it. Tap the torch on green ground in the fire's path — burnt land feeds nothing. Careful: your fire spreads too.
  • Read the wind. The arrow up top is truth. It shifts as the night wears on, and hot fires throw embers over thin lines.
  • Save every cabin. Lose one and the shift is lost. Survive all five shifts to see the dawn.

Mouse / touch to drag or tap · arrows move the cursor, space applies, Z / X swap tools · P pause · M sound · R restart
