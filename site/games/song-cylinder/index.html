The six o’clock commission

Winter, 1897. Six tunes wait on the patron’s card, and the demonstration begins at eight.

  • The card at the top shows the tune exactly: each dark hole wants one brass pin on the cylinder below. Columns are beats; rows are comb tines, high notes on top.
  • Tap a hole to set a pin, tap it again to pull it out. You can pluck-test any pin the moment you set it.
  • Short on faith? Spend a wind to hear the patron’s own barrel play the true tune. Two winds per tune.
  • Present the cylinder when it matches. The candle never stops burning, so work briskly — accuracy and candlelight both become score.
  • Miss the mark and the patron leaves without a box. Pass all six tunes and the workshop makes its name.
