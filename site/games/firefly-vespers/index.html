Firefly Vespers

Dusk is falling over the swamp and a dozen fireflies blink out of step. You are the small gold one. Every flash pulls your neighbours toward your beat — find the swarm's rhythm, hold it, and the whole marsh will breathe as one light.

  • Flash in time. Flash when others are about to blink and they swing toward you. Fill the unison meter past the gold mark and hold it.
  • Never flash alone. A frog watches for lonely flashes. Three strikes and your night is over.
  • Beat the fog. Each night ends when the fog arrives — or when the swamp blinks as one.
