Live Wire

You are a spark of current in a failing mainframe. Hold and drag along the copper trace from the START pad to the FINISH pad. Drift off the trace or clip a component and you short out - three fuses is all you get.

  • Hold & drag (or arrow keys) to ride the trace
  • Stay on the copper - edges flicker before you short
  • Time rotors, flares and solder bugs as you pass
  • Grab loose charges for bonus voltage

Mouse / touch & drag · arrows or WASD nudge · P pause · M sound · R restart

Board goal: carry the spark from START to FINISH without shorting. Clean runs build your voltage multiplier.
