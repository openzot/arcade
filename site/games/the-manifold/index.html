The Manifold

“One hose. Two men. Bring up the silver.”

Wreck of the clipper Cygnet, forty fathom down, and the glass is falling. Two hardhat divers work her decks, but the barge carries a single air line — the manifold can feed only one man at a time.

  • 1 / 2 or tap a valve pad — feed Port or Starboard diver
  • SPACE or STOKE pad — drive the boiler, fill the manifold (mind the heat!)
  • Q / E — jiggle a kinked hose clear
  • Keep each chest-gauge in its green band to work; red bursts hoses, empty suits fade men out
  • Quota raised? Hold W or HAUL to wind both men up before the gale breaks

P pause · M sound · R restart dive
