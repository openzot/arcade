Anvil Song

Five blades. One dying coal bed. Strike on the glow.

  • Strike — SPACE, ENTER, or tap the anvil the moment the sweeping mark crosses the glowing zone.
  • Bellows — hold B or the BELLOWS button to reheat the blade. Cold iron only dents.
  • Coal — everything burns it. Perfect strikes feed the fire. When it's gone, the forge is dead.
  • Quench — fill the shaping bar, then strike once more in the zone to set the edge.
