Falling Glass

You keep the harbour at Porth Annow. Six boats, six dawns, one owners’ bill of £96. Each morning read the glass, the vane and the sky, then choose how many boats make sail.

  • Click boats to ready them, or press 1–6.
  • Sail sends every ready boat to the grounds.
  • Mid-morning you may ring the recall bell once: boats come home safe but land only part of their catch.
  • Sent boats earn by the weather; gales and storms can take them. Every boat lost costs £10 to the widows’ fund.
