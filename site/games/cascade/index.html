Cascade

Closing night for Bostock & Verralli's Travelling Circus. The ring is yours: a lazy three-ball warm-up that grows, ball by ball, into a seven-ball finale under the gaslights.

  • Hold F or J — or press and hold one half of the ring — to charge that hand.
  • Release to hurl the ball across. Longer charge, higher arc.
  • Your hands catch whatever falls into them; the art is interleaving the arcs so a hand is always free.
  • Every kiss with the sawdust snuffs a lamp. Three snuffs and the act is over. Space throws from the fullest hand.
