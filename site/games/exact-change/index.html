Exact Change

You are the Saturday girl at Harbour Sweets. Every customer pays too much — count their change out of the till, exact to the penny, before their patience drains away.

  • Tap coins (or keys 1–9 and 0) to stack up their change.
  • Hand over (Enter) when the sum matches. Fast service earns tips; an unbroken streak earns more.
  • A wrong sum costs half their mood. Three walkouts and the day is done.
  • Mind the regulars: coin counters tip for the fewest coins, kids can't wait, and the afternoon brings a rush minute.
