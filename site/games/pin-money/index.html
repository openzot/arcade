Pin Money

No machine sets these pins. You do.

  • Every frame a ball thunders off the lane into your pit. Mind your shins.
  • Drag the deadwood home — any fallen pin will stand in any empty spot. Ten seated pins rack the frame.
  • Hard-hit pins split. They won't stand: carry them to the spare bin and take the fresh one it hands you.
  • The bowler's patience drains while the wood lies about. Empty it and you're sacked. Twelve fast frames earn your wage envelope.
  • Watch for nights the bowler throws twice.

Lane six, the Starlite Bowl, autumn 1959. Paid by the frame.
