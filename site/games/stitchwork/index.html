Piece each patchwork block to the commission — every patch takes the fabric it asks for — then run every seam with its called-for thread before you may set the block. The oil lamp burns while you work; set a block to refill it. Three snapped needles close the shop for the night.

  • Piece: click a tray fabric (keys 1–5, 6 scrap bag), then click a pale patch. Hovering a patch shows what it wants.
  • Sew: once the top is pieced, load a thread (Q / E) and click each dashed seam. Level seams want scarlet; standing and slant seams want steel.
  • Mind the lamp: it burns down as you dawdle. A finished block refills it; a flawless one returns a snapped needle.

How to play

  1. Piece the top. Pick a fabric from the tray (keys 1–5, 6 = scrap bag), then click a pale patch. The patch shows the fabric it wants when you hover or move onto it. A scrap bag fills any hole, but you carry only three per commission.
  2. Sew the seams. When every patch is filled, pick a thread colour (Q = scarlet, E = steel, or click a spool) and click a seam — the dashed line between two patches. Level seams want scarlet; standing and slant seams want steel. The wrong thread snaps and costs a needle.
  3. Mind the lamp. The oil burns while you work. Setting a block refills it; a flawless block also returns a needle. If the lamp gutters out it costs a needle too.
  4. Five blocks make the commission. Three snapped needles end it early.
  5. Keyboard: arrows / WASD walk between patches and seams, Space places fabric or sews, Z undoes, R redoes the current block, P pauses, M mutes.
