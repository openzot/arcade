Crossing 1 ■ 26 timber BUILDING ★ 0/15

The flood took the road.

Five culverts on the valley road washed out in the night — and the school bus is due. You are the road gang’s engineer. Bolt timber between the steel anchors, brace it into triangles, then wave the loaded bus across.

  • Drag from any joint or anchor to a grid point to lay a beam. Planks cost timber — the meter up top counts down.
  • Beams longer than one length of lumber are refused, so big gaps need triangles. Flat decks sag; trusses hold.
  • Tap a beam twice (or use Undo) to take it back. Then press Test: amber creaks, red snaps.
  • Get the bus to the far bank. Spare timber becomes stars.
