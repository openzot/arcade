Grand Pier Hotel · Night Exchange

The Exchange

You are the night operator. Guests ring down the left; plug a cord into where they want to go before their ring dies. Five cords, six lines, one busy season.

Tap a ringing guest, then a line on the right.
Keys: 1–8 pick a guest · arrows + Enter move & plug · Esc put the cord down · P pause · M sound · R new shift

Miss three rings and the manager is up the stairs. Serve twenty calls to see the dawn.
