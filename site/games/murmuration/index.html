Murmuration

Stay tight, or feed the falcon.

  • Steer the pale-tipped leader. Arrows / WASD, or hold and drag anywhere.
  • The flock follows you. A tight ball denies the peregrine — it only takes stragglers.
  • Thread every lit arch. Miss one and the dusk leaps closer.
  • Space (or the ✦ pad) pulls the flock into a panic ball when the falcon stoops.
  • Bring half the flock under the pier before full dark. Five legs to the roost.
