    Shelf of essences

      Athanor

      A thunder-lit garret above the apothecary. On the shelf: four primordials — fire, water, earth, air. In the crucible: everything else, waiting to be found.

      • Combine any two essences to attempt a synthesis.
      • Forty-two more essences hide inside the tree, up to the Philosopher's Stone.
      • Wrong pairs gutter the candle but cost nothing but pride.
      • The Whisper hint names a working pair for −20 points.
      • Chain discoveries within a minute of each other for bonus points.

      Drag an essence onto another, or tap two and press Transmute. Keys: R restart · M sound · H help.

      The Great Work is done

      The Stone sits warm in your palm. Rain still lashes the glass, the furnace still breathes — but the garret glows like noon.
