Sit at the wheel

Six commissions wait on the shelf. Each wants its own silhouette pulled out of a spinning lump of clay before the clay stiffens.

  1. Aim the rib. ↑ ↓ — or rest your finger at the height you want to shape.
  2. Press the wall. Hold ◀ to pull the clay in, ▶ to push it out. On the wheel itself, press against the outside to squeeze, inside the hollow to flare.
  3. Pull taller. Press inward and drag the rib up: the wall rises with your hand.
  4. Mind the clay. Heavy pressing shakes the pot off centre and dries it out — ease off and let the wheel settle. Hold SPACE for water; too much and it slumps. ENTER sets a finished pot aside.
