Heliostat

Midsummer, and the sun is late for the valley below. Eight chambers of bronze glass sit between you and first light — turn the mirrors, split the beam, and walk the morning where it is wanted.

  • Tap a mirror to swing it between its two angles.
  • One beam leaves the sun disc and obeys every surface it meets.
  • Light every crystal in the chamber at once.
  • Splinters of prism throw the beam sideways; stone swallows it whole.

Keyboard: arrows move the focus, Enter turns a mirror. R resets the chamber.

The Valley Wakes

Every chamber is lit, and the light goes on without you.
