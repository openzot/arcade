Beacon Ridge

Raid sails have been sighted past the point. Seven watch-fires stand cold along the downs, and the whole ridge is black as a boot. Take your ember and wake the chain — dawn will only come to a ridge that burns whole.

  • Your taper burns ~24 seconds. Touch a burning pyre and it drinks full again.
  • Hold beside a cold pyre to kindle it. Lit pyres burn down their own fuel and go dark — a cold pyre can always be lit again.
  • Gales crouch every flame: fuel drains three times as fast while the wind shoves you off your line.
  • Bog pools cling at your heels and wet the taper. Gather brushwood bundles (carry three) and feed them to a pyre to lengthen its burn.
  • All seven must burn together. If your ember dies between fires, the night takes you.
