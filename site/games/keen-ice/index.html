Keen Ice

The pond is swept, the lanterns are lit and Bessie from the next farm fancies her chances. Four ends. Most points takes the bonspiel.

  • Aim with ←/→ or by dragging the aim pad.
  • Pick an in-turn or out-turn — the handle decides which way the stone bends as it slows.
  • Hold the throw button (or SPACE) to charge, release to let go inside the amber band for true weight.
  • Sweep (SHIFT or the button) while it runs: sweeping carries the stone farther and keeps it straighter. Watch your sweeper's arms.
  • Nearest stone to the button scores one a stone. Guard it, draw round it, or knock Bessie's out of the house.

Stones that stop short of the far hog line are lifted, off the sheet are gone.
