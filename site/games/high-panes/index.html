HIGH PANES

The city looks better from up here.

You hang off a tower block in a swinging cradle with six filthy panes between you and quitting time.

Drag (or WASD / arrows) to work the squeegee and clear the grime. Wiping dry glass smears - dip the bucket (B) for fresh suds. Mind the pigeons. Reach 93% shine, then press Space to ring the bell and drop to the next pane.
