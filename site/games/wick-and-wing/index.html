Wick & Wing

You hold the matches. The moth holds the sky.

  • A moth cannot help itself: it always flies at the nearest burning wick, and the violet zappers burn brightest of all.
  • Click or tap a lantern to snuff it or relight it. Relighting costs one match; snuffing is free.
  • Lure the moth over every sleeping bud before dawn to pollinate it.
  • Keyboard: ←/→ cycle lanterns (or keys 1–9), Space snuff/light, P pause, R restart, M sound.
  • In the dark the moth coasts straight - sometimes the kindest thing is to put every light out.
