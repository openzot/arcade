Shell Market

A hermit-crab housing ladder on a moonlit flat.

  • The tide brings shells. Bigger is better — but claiming one means crossing open sand naked.
  • Every claim empties somebody's old shell. Watch vacancies ripple down the chain and steal the good ones first.
  • Gulls hunt soft crabs. Failed fits cost seconds. Surging waves shove you and drag small shells away.
  • Survive six tides to dawn owning the grandest shell on the beach.

Mouse / touch — tap a shell to scuttle to it and try it on. Keys — WASD or arrows to walk, space tries the nearest shell, P pause, M sound, R restart.
