Waterwolf

Three nights of storm. One pair of hands. Rain fills the terraced canals, and the sea will only take water back while the tide ebbs.

  • Tap a gate (or press 1 2 3) to raise or drop it.
  • Gate III is the sea sluice - open it on an ebb, shut it before the flood turns.
  • The tide table (top left) shows what the sea does next. The mill bails a trickle, never enough.
  • If a canal tops its brim the dike breaches. Lose all three ◆ seals and the polder drowns.
