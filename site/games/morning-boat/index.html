The Morning Boat

A scatter of rain-washed islands, one mail steamer, and a tide that waits for nobody.

  • Draw routes. Tap a pier, then more piers, to lay a route. Tap the first pier again to close it into a loop, press Enter to leave it open. Each route ties up one steamer, which sails it forever.
  • Match the wants. Every islander carries folk ☺, post ✉ or a crate ▣. They land happily only at an island whose badges show their mark.
  • Mind the pier. Seven waiting and the jetty grumbles; let it fill twice and you lose patience. Three lost hearts end the week.
  • Beat the tide. Deliver the day's quota before the tide bar runs out. Pass the day, choose a boon, sail on. Thursday's boat ends the week.
