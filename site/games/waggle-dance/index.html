Waggle Dance

You are the scout. The comb is your map.

A scout bee returns from the fields and dances on the vertical comb: the angle of her waggle run points the swarm at the flowers, measured against the shaft of sunlight; how long she waggles says how far.

  • Hold Space or press & hold anywhere on the comb to begin the waggle.
  • Aim: steer with ←→ or move your finger/pointer - match the faint memory-line to the patch on the radar.
  • Distance: release when the growing length-ring reaches the patch's ring.
  • Wind shoves you mid-dance - fight it. The truer the dance, the more foragers join, the faster honey fills the comb.
  • E eats 60 honey to restore energy · P pause · M sound · R restart.

Fill 600 honey before the sixth dawn ends, or the hive winters empty.
