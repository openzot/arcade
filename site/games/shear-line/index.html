Shear Line

Feel, don’t force.

  • Slide the pick along the keyway — drag left and right (or ← →).
  • Lift each pin by dragging up (↑ / ↓). Every pin stops at its own height — listen for the float, watch for the glow.
  • Hold torsion while a pin floats to shear it — keep Space or the TORSION pad pressed. Feather it: lean too long and the plug binds.
  • Set all five pins and the plug turns. Five stubborn heirloom locks stand between you and the closing bell.
  • A slipped pin or a bound plug costs two seconds of the clock.
