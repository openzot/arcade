TALKDOWN

Eight tired flights, one wet runway, and you are the voice on the scope.

  1. Tap a blip to call that flight up.
  2. Tap anywhere on the scope and it turns toward that point. Climb or descend with the buttons or the arrow keys.
  3. Deliver each flight onto the dashed centreline below 3,600 ft, heading west. The box flies the approach itself.

Hold three miles or one thousand feet apart. Dry tanks are a strike — three strikes and you are relieved.
