Bone Bed

A skeleton sleeps under sixty million years of rock.

  • Pick chews through rock fast — but every swing that lands on bone cracks it.
  • You will glimpse bone just before you break through: switch to the Brush and free it gently.
  • Brushed-clean bones raise your completeness. Cracked ones lower your fossil integrity.
  • Probe pings a patch of ground and ghosts any bone hiding in it. You carry five.
  • Excavate the whole skeleton intact to win. Lose integrity and the specimen is ruined.

Mouse / touch: hold on the ground to work · 1 / 2 / 3 or tap to change tool · P pause · R restart · M sound
