Quiet Hours

The Grand Ellery closes at nine. At nine-oh-one you are already inside. Three pieces belong to you tonight — lift them all and slip out through the service stair before the watch makes you.

Move — WASD / arrows, or hold the left pad on touch
Lift a piece — stand close and press E / Space or the LIFT button
Hide — dark pools swallow their lantern light; stand still to vanish
Guards — their cone of light finds movement and shine. Caught twice and the night is over.
