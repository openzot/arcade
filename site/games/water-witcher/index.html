Five fields. One hazel rod. A stake that will not stretch.

The Water Witcher

The dry summer of 1921 has cracked Miss Hedley's hop garden to hardpan. She has heard you carry the old gift, so take the rod and find what is left of the water — before her money, or your luck, runs out.

  • Walk the rows with WASD / arrow keys, or press and drag on the field.
  • Watch the rod. The harder it dips and the faster it ticks, the closer the water lies. When it wrenches sideways, it is pointing.
  • Sink a borehole where you stand — Space, or tap the DRILL pad. £3 a shot. A wet bore pays by the litre; a dry one drinks your stake.
  • Raise each field's quota in litres to open the gate to the next. Empty stake with the quota unmet ends the season.
